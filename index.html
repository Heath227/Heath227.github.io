Saternetes sync - privacy

Saternetes sync is a private tool run by Saternetes Ltd. It signs in with Google to read Google Docs that the signed-in user can already open, and copies them to a computer controlled by Saternetes for internal use.

It does not sell, share or publish your Google data, and it does not change or delete your documents.

To remove access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: github.com/saternetes
